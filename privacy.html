Wend Privacy Policy

Effective: October 24, 2026

Wend is made by Yusuf Günay (Günay Studio), Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: {CONTACT_EMAIL}

What Wend does not do

Wend has no accounts and shows no ads. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Location

Wend uses your device location to draw routes around you and to record walks you start. Route planning happens on your phone. Your journal is stored on your phone.

Walks you choose to share

If you turn on Share walks, Wend uploads each finished walk without your name or account: the path with its first and last 200 meters removed, where you paused, where you left the suggested route, distance, duration, the hour it started (rounded) and your country. We use this only to improve Wend's maps and routes. You can turn sharing off at any time and delete your shared walks in Wend > Privacy. Shared walks are kept for up to 36 months.

Usage and crash data

Wend collects how the app is used (for example, which features are opened and whether routes are completed), crash reports and basic device information, linked to a random identifier created by the app. You can turn this off in Wend > Privacy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We receive purchase status and a random customer identifier from our purchase service so your subscription works across reinstalls. We never see your payment details.

Health

If you allow it, Wend writes finished walks to Health Connect or Apple Health on your phone. Wend does not read your health data and never uploads it.

Service providers

Google Firebase (anonymous sign-in, analytics, crash reports, storage of shared walks), RevenueCat (subscription status), and map data services that receive the map area being loaded (OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap services, Open-Meteo for weather). They process data on our behalf.

Your rights

You can access, delete or stop the collection of your data in the app, or by writing to {CONTACT_EMAIL}. Residents of California, the EU/EEA, the UK and Türkiye (KVKK) have these rights under their laws; we honor them for everyone.

Children

Wend is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.
